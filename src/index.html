Common questions

Straight answers
Ask us anything → We usually reply within one business day.
01 How long does it take to build a website?

Most projects are completed within 4–5 weeks, depending on scope and how quickly feedback comes back to us. Larger or more complex sites may take longer — we'll give you a realistic timeline up front.

02 Do you offer ongoing support or maintenance?

Yes. Our maintenance packages keep your site secure, up to date, and running smoothly after launch. Ask us about ongoing support options when we scope your project.

03 What if I need changes after the website goes live?

Minor revisions within the agreed scope are included. For anything beyond that, we offer affordable hourly or retainer-based support — no gatekeeping, no surprise invoices.

04 Who hosts the website?

Your website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, connected directly to a GitHub repository. When we make updates, Cloudflare automatically detects the change, builds the project, and deploys it live — all within minutes.
